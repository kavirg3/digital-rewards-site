Questions Owners Ask Us.

What exactly does Digital Rewards do?

We design, set up and run a digital loyalty programme for your business, plus the campaigns that bring customers back.

Do I need an app?

No. Your loyalty card is digital and lives on your customer's phone.

How do customers join?

Usually by scanning a QR code in your store or tapping a link, then filling in a short form.

Do customers need to download anything?

In most cases no. The card is saved to the wallet already on their phone, where their device supports it.

Does it work with Apple Wallet and Google Wallet?

Yes, where the platform and the customer's device support it. We confirm this during setup.

Can I use points, stamps and cashback?

Yes. We recommend what suits your business and confirm it in your proposal.

Can you manage the programme for me?

Yes. Growth and Premium include our management; Starter is self-managed.

How much does it cost?

From R299 a month. Growth is R1,499 and Premium is R2,499. Enterprise is priced on request. Pricing is indicative until confirmed in your proposal.

Can I use it across multiple branches?

Yes. Tell us how many branches you have and we will scope and price it.

How do I know whether it is working?

We set a baseline, then report on sign-ups, repeat visits, rewards redeemed and campaign responses.

How long does implementation take?

It depends on your branding and branches. You get a timeline after your assessment.

Can I use my existing branding?

Yes. Your card is designed with your logo and colours.

Can I integrate it with my current POS system?

It depends on your POS. We check compatibility first. The programme can also run alongside your POS without an integration.
